# knomee.com homepage — the exact content

Every word on the prototype pages comes from the live knomee.com homepage
(captured 2026-10-22), copied character for character: curly quotes, em dashes,
capitals and typos included. The redesign changes layout, visuals and motion
only. Nothing from other knomee.com pages is used.

Two deliberate omissions, both from the organization rule against customer PII:
- Customers' staff names are dropped from their quotes; the rest of each
  attribution stays as written. Endorsers and the research partner keep theirs.
- Customer headshots are not used.

Text that appears **inside product mockups** (questions, scores, names in a
prospect list) is product UI, drawn as imagery, and every person in it is
invented. No marketing copy is written for the mockups.
