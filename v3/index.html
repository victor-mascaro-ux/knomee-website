1. What is Knomee?

Knomee turns behavioral intelligence into organic growth, helping advisors identify opportunities, convert prospects, and deepen client relationships.

2. How is Knomee different from traditional growth and discovery tools?

Traditional growth tools help you find more leads. Traditional discovery tools help advisors see what prospects already know. Knomee does both differently, creating valuable digital experiences that reveal who’s ready to act, what matters, and how to engage them to drive growth.

3. What does Knomee help me understand about clients and prospects?

Knomee reveals what matters most, what’s changing, what’s driving decisions, what may be holding someone back, and how ready they are to act, including insights that often don’t surface in traditional financial conversations.

4. How does Knomee help me grow my practice?

Knomee helps you increase close rates, close prospects faster, grow wallet share, and retain relationships through transitions by revealing who’s ready to act, what matters, and what’s changing, then surfacing conversation starters in their own words so they feel understood and motivated to act.

5. Is Knomee complicated or time-consuming to use?

Not at all. Knomee fits into your existing workflow and takes minutes to get started. Clients and prospects complete an engaging experience in under 10 minutes, while advisors receive actionable insights and conversation starters without adding more work to their day.

6. How do I use Knomee in my workflow?

Knomee adapts to how you already go to market, whether engagement is driven by a centralized marketing, business development or growth team, or directly by advisors. Our Activation Toolkit provides proven workflows, templates and guidance for prospecting, onboarding, client reviews, household engagement and transitions, making it easy to launch quickly, drive adoption and start seeing value immediately.
